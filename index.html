1.3.1 BETA · live

What's new in this deck

  • Debate Matrix rebuilt64 curated sheets (up from 19), a 20-rule interaction matrix, round-by-round reads, win conditions for both sides, difficulty ratings and an auditable score breakdown.
  • New commands“A vs B vs C” ranks a battle royale, plus “tier list”, “top 10”, “random” and “Naruto roster”. Long-press nothing — it is all typed input.
  • NormalPlain conversation with no persona: questions, a real arithmetic evaluator, planning help, jokes, and an honest “I don't know” when it doesn't.
  • Spicy · 18+Flirty banter and adult humour behind a one-time age gate. Suggestive at most, never explicit, and it stops the moment you ask it to.
  • Roast PitPlayful roasting, comebacks and backhanded hype. It goes after what you do, never who you are, and backs off if it lands badly.
  • Conspiracy CornerAbsurd theories with total confidence and zero evidence. Ask “is this true” and it breaks character immediately.
  • Two personas retiredAcademic Assistant and Pretzel Chef are gone, replaced by the four above. The settings tab now lists all five new personas.
  • Catalog and designCarried over from 1.3: 56 games, the New shelf, genre chips, paged cards and per-title cover art.
  • Where this build lives1.3.1 BETA is published to GitHub Pages. Patch notes inside Settings carry the full list.

18+ gate

Spicy mode is for adults

This persona is flirty, uses adult humour and talks about dating like an adult. Nothing explicit, no images — tone, not content.

  • What it doesInnuendo, compliments, date ideas, jokes, and calling you out when your flirting is bad.
  • What it won't doExplicit sexual content, anything involving minors, or anything you can't talk your way out of with “stop”.
  • What this gate isA one-time confirmation saved in this browser. It is a tone check, not proof of age and not real security.

Confirm you are 18 or older. You can leave the mode at any time from the persona list.
